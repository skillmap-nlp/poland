AI for Managers — ChatGPT Workshops and Designing Custom AI Assistants

English translation of the original Polish course description published on uslugirozwojowe.parp.gov.pl (BUR).

BUR ID: 2147461  ·  Provider: Lower Silesian Regional Development Agency (DARR S.A.)  ·  Voivodeship: Dolnośląskie  ·  Category: IT and Telecommunications  ·  Duration: 16 hours (2 days)
Educational goal

The goal of the training is to equip managers with the competences required to effectively implement and use AI in their organisations, with an emphasis on the practical applications of ChatGPT and on designing custom AI assistants.

Programme
Day 1 — Introduction to AI in business and overview of AI tools

Module I — Course introduction

  • Welcome and participant introductions
  • Presentation of training goals and agenda
  • Pre-test of AI knowledge

Module II — Foundations of AI in business

  • Introduction to artificial intelligence and its applications
  • Overview of AI tools available to managers
  • Benefits of using AI in management and business

Module III — The LLM revolution: ChatGPT and AI assistants

  • Introduction to ChatGPT — capabilities and limitations
  • Demonstration of practical applications of ChatGPT in management
  • Comparison of ChatGPT with other available AI tools

Module IV — Workshop: practical application of ChatGPT

  • Hands-on exercises: using ChatGPT in a manager's daily work
  • Case study: solving business problems with ChatGPT
  • Discussion and exchange of experiences
Day 2 — Designing and implementing AI assistants

Module V — Principles of designing effective AI assistants

  • Key components of an effective AI assistant
  • Designing a conversational user interface
  • Analysing business needs and matching them to AI features

Module VI — Workshop: building individual AI assistants

  • Hands-on exercises: designing your own AI assistants
  • Simulating and testing the designed solutions
  • Feedback and optimisation of participants' projects

Module VII — Advanced ChatGPT features and integration with business systems

  • Advanced use of ChatGPT (e.g. data analysis, report generation)
  • Integration of ChatGPT with other business tools and systems
  • Case studies of effective AI implementations in organisations

Module VIII — Course wrap-up

  • Post-test of acquired knowledge
  • Summary of the training and key take-aways
  • Award of attendance certificates
